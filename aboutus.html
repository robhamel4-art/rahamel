About TKR Media

Built on Creativity. Driven by Passion. TKR Media was founded by Robert Hamel, a music educator and entrepreneur with a passion for creativity and bringing ideas to life. What began as a creative outlet while balancing two jobs and full-time college classes grew into something much more. Through professional development courses and business studies at Arizona State University, TKR Media developed into a business that fulfilled the capstone requirements for Robert's business degree. Today, TKR Media brings together custom products, laser engraving, and independent music promotion. Whether creating something unique or helping musicians share their sound, the mission remains the same: to inspire creativity and support the passions of others. Because creativity shouldn't have to wait until life slows down.
